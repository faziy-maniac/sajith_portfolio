The Athlete & The Vision

Bridging elite athletic performance with analytical engineering discipline.

"In football, victory is won in the spaces between lines. In coding, great software is engineered in the logic between functions."

I am a professional footballer who thrives on the intensity, rapid decision-making, and discipline demanded at the highest level of sport. Operating from the midfield, my game is centered on reading plays before they unfold, dictating rhythm, and putting the team's victory above everything else.

Alongside my football career, I've embarked on a serious journey into computer science and software development. The synergy is natural: debugging code requires the exact same patience as breaking down a low defensive block, while architecting software mirrors the tactical organization of a matchday squad. I bring relentless curiosity, mental toughness, and a collaborative spirit wherever I play or build.
